# Changelog – Victron ESS Node-RED Manager (English edition)

Newest entry first. The English edition starts at 0.9.054; the full history of earlier versions
is in Czech (cs/CHANGELOG.txt in the repository).


## 0.9.072 – 2026-11-01

- Mobile app: a running battery calibration is highlighted - red vertical bar, light red background
  and bold text (only while calibration is running).

## 0.9.071 – 2026-11-01

- Mobile app: a Statistics table at the bottom as on the dashboard Overview - estimated earnings
  from selling, PV savings and the total per day, week (7 days) and month (30 days).

## 0.9.070 – 2026-11-01

- **Fix: winter calibration never charges at the high tariff.** In 0.9.065, after 3 nights in LT
  the calibration day started and topped up from the grid to 100 % even after LT ended. In winter
  calibration now charges only in LT: with spot, the first 3 nights only in quarter-hours with spot
  at or below the day's average, then in every LT quarter-hour until the battery reaches 100 %. The
  calibration day and the afternoon top-up above 95 % are not used in winter. Summer unchanged.
- Statistics on the Overview: table with borders.

## 0.9.069 – 2026-11-01

- **Relays and Shelly - "Automatic - by price" mode with a switching plan.** New field "Switch by":
  lowest spot price / low tariff (LT) / below a set price (+ field "Set price"), plus "Minimum run time
  (h)" and "Start from". From the "Start from" time the switching quarter-hours are planned for 24
  hours: for spot the cheapest quarter-hours adding up to the minimum run time, for LT all LT
  quarter-hours, for the set price all quarter-hours up to it; if that is not enough for the minimum
  run time, the cheapest others are added. Always on at a negative price. Prices include HT/LT fees.
  The Current states table shows when a switched-off device will switch on.
- Earnings and savings statistics are calculated every 3 minutes (previously every minute).

## 0.9.068 – 2026-11-01

- **Sell from the battery only when it pays off.** New field "Battery wear (CZK/kWh)" on the Tariffs
  and prices page (roughly battery price / (capacity x cycles x 0.8), default 0). The battery is
  sold (mechanism A and planned selling B) only at a sell price of at least (refill cost / 0.85) +
  wear. Refill cost: in winter the cheapest buy price in the next 24 h, in summer the lowest sell
  price in the next 24 h (PV refills the battery). Selling PV surplus is unchanged. When selling
  does not pay off, the Battery selling plan row on the Overview says so.
- **Relays and Shelly: mode "Automatic - by price".** Switches on when electricity is cheap (buy
  price incl. fees at or below the day's average, or negative). Relays also only within the set time
  window. The existing mode is now called "Automatic - surplus and SOC"; its thresholds are hidden in
  the other modes.
- **Overview - Statistics** (formerly Today's statistics): instead of "kWh exported today" (already
  in the energy row) an estimate of earnings from selling, savings from PV self-consumption and the
  total - per day, 7 days and 30 days. Calculated every minute from the Victron counters and current
  prices, stored in a file (survives a restart).
- **E-mail notifications** (own-token installations only): calibration start and end, fallback
  calibration day, required SOC will not be reached, spot prices missing, Victron without data for
  over 10 minutes.

## 0.9.067 – 2026-11-01

- Shorter, simpler (i) help texts for all long fields on the Overview, Setup, Modes and Tariffs
  pages - a few sentences without technical terms. Details (reason codes, safety nets,
  calculations) are in the README. Control logic unchanged.

## 0.9.066 – 2026-11-01

- Shorter, simpler help text for battery calibration (the detailed description stays in the README).

## 0.9.065 – 2026-11-01

- **Winter calibration in the low tariff.** In the winter profile (automatically) the battery is not
  topped up in the afternoon at the high tariff during calibration, but charged to 100 % in the low
  tariff: with a fixed buy price in any LT quarter-hour, with spot only in LT quarter-hours with spot
  at or below the day's average (an evening LT during a price peak is skipped). It charges at the set
  max. charging power in the nearest suitable LT quarter-hours; what does not fit into one night is
  charged the next night. The calibration day with daytime top-up starts only on day 4 of the
  calibration mode (safety net). The afternoon top-up above the SOC threshold (default 95 %) stays.
  Summer behaviour unchanged. The Overview grid charging plan shows "calibration 100 % in LT (...)".
- In calibration mode planned selling from the battery (B) does not sell even during grid charging.

## 0.9.064 – 2026-10-26

- Fix: the Venus OS version in the dashboard header disappeared after importing a new flow version
  and deploying (without restarting Node-RED) - it was only sent on change. It is now also sent
  every 15 s.

## 0.9.063 – 2026-10-26

- Plain-language setting labels for non-technical users: field names say what the field does,
  without SOC/setpoint/mechanism A-B-C jargon (e.g. "Battery reserve for the house only", "Hold at the
  energy meter", "Always charge from grid to at least"). Technical
  details and the original technical names stay in the (i) help texts, which now start with a
  simple sentence. Price type options "Fixed price (fix)" / "Spot price". Control logic and saved
  configuration unchanged.

## 0.9.062 – 2026-10-26

  imported from the grid and PV production (kWh from the Victron counters).

## 0.9.061 – 2026-10-26

- English edition: remaining Czech words translated in the grid charging plan ("minimum", "cheap",
  "from") and in the morning deferral state ("X of Y quarter-hours qualify").
- Screenshots in the GitHub README (demo installation).

## 0.9.060 – 2026-10-26

- Mobile app: a line with the flow version and the Venus OS version at the bottom.

## 0.9.059 – 2026-10-26

- Currency follows the market zone everywhere on the dashboard: prices on the Overview, the spot
  price chart (grid and labels), plan and morning deferral messages, labels of price fields
  (CZK/kWh or EUR/kWh). EUR prices with 3 decimals; price fields have a 0.001 step.
- The mobile app shows prices in the installation's currency.
- The README page starts with a link to GitHub.

## 0.9.058 – 2026-10-26

- Spot prices for other European markets: new "Market (zone)" selection on the Tariffs and prices
  page. CZ is unchanged (spotovaelektrina.cz, OTE backup, CZK). Other zones (DE-LU, AT, PL, SK, HU,
  NL, BE, FR, IT, Nordic, Baltic...) take prices in EUR from Energy-Charts.
- Local time follows the zone (windows, tariffs, price quarter-hours, Open-Meteo forecast),
  including daylight saving time.
- When the currency changes (CZK <-> EUR), the price settings are converted at 25 CZK/EUR.
- The heartbeat also reports the market zone.

## 0.9.054 – 2026-10-25
