# Changelog – Victron ESS Node-RED Manager (English edition)

Newest entry first. The English edition starts at 0.9.054; the full history of earlier versions
is in Czech (cs/CHANGELOG.txt in the repository).


## 0.9.055 – 2026-10-26

- Overview: "Current plan" split into two rows - "Battery selling plan" and "Grid charging plan",
  each with its own help. The mobile app shows the plan on two lines as well.
- Mobile app in English: the language follows the flow edition (English flow = English app), before
  the first data arrive the phone language. The support page now also shows the IBAN.

## 0.9.054 – 2026-10-25

- First English edition of the flow (flow-en.json): all dashboard texts, help texts, status and
  plan messages in English; regulation logic identical to the Czech edition.
- English user guide (README page in the dashboard).
- Calibration counts days by calendar date (a 25-hour day at the end of daylight saving time no
  longer counts as an extra day).
